Interest Track

Interest & Curiosity Profiling

Discover what captivates a child's imagination, drives their exploratory energy, and reveals natural affinities across arts, sciences, and teamwork.

1. Creative & Visual Arts

Observes preference for visual storytelling, color theory, music, imaginative crafting, and independent creative expression.

Drawing • Storycraft • Performing Arts

2. Scientific & STEM Inquiry

Explores intrinsic interest in biological ecosystems, astronomical wonders, coding mechanics, robotics, and hands-on experiments.

Nature • Technology • Experiments

3. Social & Collaborative

Measures comfort with collaborative games, community projects, empathetic listening, leadership, and group dialogue.

Teamwork • Empathy • Peer Leadership

Sample Scenario Prompt Preview

Interest profiling uses scenario prompts where all options are valued equally, emphasizing genuine interest over performance pressure:

Sample Scenario • Weekend Discovery
"Imagine you have a free Saturday to invent or create anything you wish in a community workshop. Which activity would you choose first?"
1. Build a motorized robot or model solar system.
2. Paint a mural or write an illustrated fantasy comic book.
3. Organize a collaborative board game tournament for friends.
4. Plant an outdoor herb garden and record insect behaviors.

Scoring dynamically maps answers to multi-axis interest profiles without judging right or wrong.
