Assessment Hub

Structured Child Assessment Program

MindBloom combines cognitive pattern analysis with interest profiling to create a balanced, holistic view of each child's individual learning capabilities.

Part 1 • Cognitive

Aptitude Evaluation

Explores how the child processes structural relationships, deduce sequences, calculates quantitative patterns, and visualizes geometric orientations.

  • Logical Reasoning: Shape progression and deductive reasoning
  • Numerical Ability: Arithmetic logic and number sense
  • Spatial Visualization: 2D to 3D rotation and visual puzzles
Est. Duration: ~15 mins Explore Aptitude Details
Part 2 • Passions

Interest Profiling

Discovers what activities, environments, and questions spark the child's genuine curiosity and sustained enthusiasm outside of strict classroom tasks.

  • Creative Thinking: Visual imagination, music, and crafting
  • Scientific Inquiry: Natural science, coding, and experiments
  • Social & Communication: Peer collaboration and storytelling
Est. Duration: ~10 mins Explore Interest Details

How to Prepare Your Child

To ensure a positive and reflective experience, keep the following guidelines in mind:

1. Low-Stress Environment

Present this as a fun, curious exploration rather than a formal school test or grading exam.

2. Self-Paced Pacing

Children are encouraged to take all the time they need and may take breaks between sections.

3. No "Wrong" Passions

In interest sections, all preferences are valid. Authenticity is celebrated above any expectation.
